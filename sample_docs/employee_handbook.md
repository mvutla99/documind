# Brightleaf Solar Employee Handbook (fictional sample)

Brightleaf Solar is a fictional company used to demonstrate DocuMind. None of these policies are real.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. PTO accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month. Part-time employees receive PTO prorated to their scheduled hours.

Up to 5 unused PTO days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31, after which they expire. PTO requests longer than three consecutive days should be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance and approved by your manager.

In addition to PTO, Brightleaf observes 10 paid company holidays each year. The holiday calendar is published in the HR portal every December.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 8 days of paid sick leave per year, separate from PTO. Sick leave does not carry over. For absences longer than three consecutive working days, a note from a healthcare provider is required.

## Parental Leave

Brightleaf offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all parents following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Leave can be taken all at once or split into up to two blocks within the first 12 months.

## Remote and Hybrid Work

Most roles at Brightleaf are hybrid. Hybrid employees are expected to work from the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays, which are team collaboration days. Fully remote arrangements require approval from both your manager and the HR business partner.

Remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $600 for equipment such as a desk, chair, or monitor, plus a monthly internet stipend of $50. Employees working remotely must be reachable during core hours, 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone.

## Expenses and Reimbursement

Business expenses must be submitted in the Expensify app within 30 days of the purchase, with an itemized receipt attached. Expenses over $75 require a receipt; smaller expenses only need a description.

Travel must be booked through the company travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under six hours. The daily meal allowance while traveling is $75. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client events.

Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll cycle after manager approval.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Each review includes a self-assessment, manager feedback, and goals for the next cycle. Salary adjustments are made once a year, effective January 1.

## Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, certifications, conferences, and books. Requests are approved by your manager. Employees may also take up to 3 learning days per year to attend training during work hours.
