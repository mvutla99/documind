# SunPeak 400 Solar Panel: Customer FAQ (fictional sample)

The SunPeak 400 is a fictional product used to demonstrate DocuMind.

## What is the power output of the SunPeak 400?

The SunPeak 400 is a 400-watt monocrystalline solar panel with a module efficiency of 21.5 percent. It measures 1,722 by 1,134 millimeters and weighs 21.5 kilograms.

## What warranty comes with the panel?

Every SunPeak 400 includes a 25-year product warranty that covers defects in materials and workmanship. It also has a 30-year performance warranty that guarantees at least 87 percent of the original power output after 30 years. Warranty claims are filed online with the panel's serial number and proof of purchase.

## Can I install the panels myself?

Do-it-yourself installation is possible for off-grid systems, but grid-tied systems must be installed by a certified Brightleaf installer to keep the warranty valid. A typical residential installation of 20 panels takes one to two days.

## How do I clean the panels?

Rain usually keeps panels clean enough. In dusty areas, rinse them with a garden hose early in the morning or in the evening, when the glass is cool. Do not use pressure washers, abrasive brushes, or harsh detergents, as they can damage the anti-reflective coating.

## How do the panels perform in snow and heat?

The SunPeak 400 is certified for snow loads up to 5,400 pascals and wind loads up to 2,400 pascals. Output drops by about 0.34 percent for every degree Celsius above 25 degrees, which is better than average for panels in its class.

## What monitoring is available?

When paired with the Brightleaf Hub inverter, the SunPeak 400 reports its output to the Brightleaf mobile app every 5 minutes. The app shows daily production, savings, and alerts if a panel underperforms.

## What is the return policy?

Uninstalled panels in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. A 15 percent restocking fee applies to opened packaging. Installed panels cannot be returned, but they remain covered by the warranty.
